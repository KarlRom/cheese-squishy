# Recorded sound source

“Slime” by **qubodup** (2011): a real recording of sticky slime in a plastic bowl.

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/127449/
- Author: https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/

- Downloaded source preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/127/127449_71257-hq.mp3

`sticky-foley.wav` contains twelve excerpts, converted to mono 22,050 Hz PCM,
with gentle fixed high/low-pass filtering, individual peak normalization and
short edge fades. Original excerpt start times are preserved in `clips.json`.
No synthetic pitch sweeps, oscillators, or resonant effects are added.

This is recorded slime foley used as a tactile sound approximation, not a
recording of the depicted cheese toy or a particular brand of petroleum jelly.
